# DELE B1 practice brief

Stage 2.18.3; verified 2026-10-07.

Sources: [current Cervantes B1 exam page](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/examenes/b1); [official B1 guide](https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/guia_examen_dele_b1.pdf), 2014 edition, pp. 6, 8–18, 24–28, 35–37.

Official: Reading 70 minutes / 5 tareas / 30 items; Listening 40 minutes / 5 tareas / 30 items, twice; Writing 60 minutes, 100–120 and 130–150 words; Speaking 15 minutes plus 15 preparation, four tareas (presentation, topic conversation, photo with follow-up, simulated dialogue). Each paper contributes 25 points. Certification requires 30/50 in BOTH groups: Reading+Writing and Listening+Speaking. Practice does not calculate certification.

All 16 parts are mapped to supported interactions. Objective tasks retain six questions each and raw /30 totals. Matching uses common banks; the three-text reading task permits reuse. Writing shows four analytic 0–3 scales, /12 per task. Speaking shows three transcript-visible analytic scales, /9 per task. Band 2 represents B1. Official holism, weighting, acoustic fluency and /25 conversions are not reproduced by these formative scores. Per-part practice timers are allocations rather than official task administration.

Implementation scope for the Spanish B1 stage requested in this chat: solo fixed examiner cue cards, written scenes for photo tasks and one assigned writing option per exercise. These follow the established practice conventions used for the preceding English and Polish B1 stages. Independent speaking tareas repeat their topic/scene context rather than depending on another stored exercise. They cannot assess actual turn-taking with an examiner. No mock product is implied. Review prompts use band 2 for expected B1 performance and reserve band 3 for stronger, consistently effective responses; criterion scores remain formative.

All seeds are original and not official examination questions. No seed WAVs, mock factory or mock overview. Spanish Kokoro metadata declares known voices but does not install them; unavailable audio generation remains explicit. Manual transcripts remain supported.

The [official 2013 reading Tarea 4](https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/b1_cl_t4.pdf) provides six gaps and eight options. The [listening Tarea 4 sample](https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/b1_ca_t4.pdf) prints ten statements, including one used by its worked example. This practice task omits the worked example and supplies the nine eligible statements for six speakers. Bank sizes are sample conventions; the guide does not fix them for every examination.
