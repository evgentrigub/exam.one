# Cambridge B1 Preliminary practice brief

Stage 2.18.1; sources verified 2026-10-07. B1 is Preliminary, not A2 Key.

Source: [Cambridge official exam format](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/preliminary/format/), sections Reading, Writing, Listening and Speaking (2020 format retained in current digital and paper exams).

Official format: Reading 45 minutes, 6 parts / 32 items (5,5,5,5,6,6); Writing 45 minutes, two tasks of about 100 words; Listening 30 minutes, 4 parts / 25 items (7,6,6,6), twice; Speaking 10–12 minutes per pair, four parts. Each component contributes 25%.

Practice scope: all 16 parts, one original seed per part, generator prompts, and model-answer/review prompts for writing and speaking. Reading and listening retain official counts and raw marks. Writing uses the four official 0–5 criteria. The 90–110-word range is app guidance around “about 100”, without automatic zero. Speaking uses four transcript-visible formative criteria, equally weighted per part, excluding Pronunciation and official weighted aggregation. No official scale or pass result is produced.

Implementation scope for the first pack requested in this chat: solo fixed examiner/partner cards; written scenes instead of photographs and visual options; one assigned article or story per exercise instead of the exam choice. These established practice conventions were stated when implementation resumed. These are language practice, not a complete simulation. Part 3 cannot assess actual negotiation with a partner. Its Interactive Communication criterion evaluates responses to supplied cues rather than live turn-taking. Part timings within Reading/Listening/Writing are practice allocations; official component timings remain above.

All bundled tasks are original, independent of copyrighted official question text. Individual practice listening seeds contain transcripts and generate audio on demand. British Kokoro voice metadata does not install a model. Manual transcripts remain available.

## Mock exams (2026-10-10)

Bundled Set 1 adds 16 fresh exam-only tasks, separate from the practice catalog. The mock combines Reading, Writing, Listening and Speaking in that order, with 45/45/30/12-minute paper timers, all 16 existing task formats, saved progress, task locking, module reviews and an optional AI overview. Generated sets use the same prompts and resumable background jobs. Set 1's 15 WAVs are generated with `pnpm generate:listening-audio --packs cambridge-en-b1`; after preparation, playback needs no runtime speech service or LLM. Writing and speaking grading still require the optional LLM; unavailable grades leave the overall result pending and answers saved.

Mock raw maxima are 32/40/25/80. Estimated Cambridge English Scale scores use the B1 anchors on p. 4 of the [official practice-test conversion guide](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/my/images/210434-converting-practice-test-scores-to-cambridge-english-scale-scores.pdf), interpolated linearly, with app-estimated raw-maximum endpoints at 170. Speaking normalizes the adapted /80 transcript rubric to /30 before conversion. The rounded average passes at 140, with Grade A from 160 (B2), B from 153 (B1), C from 140 (B1), and A2 from 120, following the [official result bands](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/images/understanding-your-statement-of-results-preliminary.pdf).

These original tasks are uncalibrated, and the speaking conversion does not reproduce pronunciation or official criterion weighting. The UI and overview label results as estimates. Mocks retain the solo speaking, written scene and assigned writing adaptations above; they do not award certification.
