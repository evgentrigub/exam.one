# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

exam.one aims to be a welcoming community for learners and contributors of different backgrounds, languages, experience levels, and abilities. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to treat others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively, and explain disagreements with specific examples.
- Be patient with language differences, unfamiliarity with the project, and honest mistakes.
- Respect others' privacy, time, and boundaries.
- Accept reasonable moderation requests and take responsibility for correcting harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in a review.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces maintained by the exam.one project. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in a public setting.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns privately using the email address in the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability). Use the subject "Code of Conduct concern" and include the relevant links or context. Share only the personal information necessary to understand the concern.

Reports are handled as privately as practical. Information may be shared with people needed to investigate or respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

The project maintainer handles reports. If a report concerns the maintainer, explain that in the message. A separate independent review process is not currently offered.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate steps to protect the community. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban.

Maintainers consider available evidence, context, severity, and prior behavior. Serious cases may require immediate restrictions. Affected participants may request reconsideration through the reporting contact.

This policy also applies to maintainers. Good-faith reports and respectful disagreement are welcome.
